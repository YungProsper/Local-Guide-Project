About Moore Square Park

Moore Square Park is a historic public park located in downtown Raleigh, North Carolina since 1792. It offers a beautiful green space for relaxation, recreation, and community events.

Things To Do

  • Walking Paths
  • Fun Activities
  • Playgrounds
  • Restaurants
  • Outdoor Concerts
  • Picnic Areas
  • Community Events

Visitors can enjoy walking paths, a playground for children, picnic areas, restaurants, various community events, and etc throughout the year. This is a place you and your family would love to go!

Plan Your Visit

Moore Square Park is located in the heart of downtown Raleigh, North Carolina. It is easily accessible by car, public transportation, and on foot.

Address: 226 S Blount St, Raleigh, NC 27601

Hours: Open daily from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM
